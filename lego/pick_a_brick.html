LEGO Pick a Brick Guide

  1. Create the Pick a Brick CSV
    1. In Bricklink Studio > File > Export As > Export as PartsList...
    2. Load the generated CSV on the bottom of this page, and click on Update CSV.
    3. Check the output (summary, skipped lines, updated lines).
  2. Submit on Lego Pick a Brick
    1. Go to LEGO > Shop > Bricks and pieces > Pick a Brick
    2. Upload List > Drag & drop the updated .csv file.
    3. Check unavailable pieces if any.
  3. Find updated version of unavailable pieces
    1. On Pick a Brick
      1. Check with keywords or IDs.
    2. On Rebrickable Parts
      1. Enter the ID of the missing piece > Check replacement parts (bottom of the page)
    3. On Bricklink
      1. Enter the ID of the missing piece > Check Alternate Item No (top of the page)
    4. In this script, update missingElementIdDict and/or replaceElementIdDict accordingly.
