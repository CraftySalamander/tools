LEGO Pick a Brick Guide

  1. Create the Pick a Brick CSV
    1. Go to Rebrickable > Connect and go to My LEGO > My Part Lists
    2. Add new Part List > Import/Delete Parts > Drag & drop the .io project > Append Parts > Close
    3. Click on created part list > Export Parts > LEGO Pick-A-Brick CSV
  2. Submit on Lego Pick a Brick
    1. Go to LEGO > Shop > Bricks and pieces > Pick a Brick
    2. Upload List > Drag & drop the .csv file
    3. Check unavailable pieces if any
  3. Find updated version of unavailable pieces
    1. On rebrickable
      1. Rebrickable Parts
      2. Enter the ID of the missing piece > Check replacement parts (bottom of the page)
    2. On Bricklink
      1. Bricklink
      2. Enter the ID of the missing piece > Check Alternate Item No (top of the page)
    3. Check if the piece is available on Pick a Brick
      1. Go to Pick a Brick
      2. Enter the new ID
    4. If no alternative was found with 1. or 2., check Pick a Brick with keywords
